Website audit finding

Contact field has no clear associated label

Priority: Medium — validate on the current production page before making a client claim.

Why it matters

Some keyboard and assistive-technology users may not know what belongs in the field. A clear label can also reduce input friction for everyone.

Recommended fix

Associate a visible label with a unique input id, preserve the design, and re-test keyboard focus, mobile layout and form submission.
